# Weave

> Weave (Weave Communications, Inc., NYSE: WEAV) is an all-in-one customer communication and payments platform for small and medium healthcare and services businesses (dental, optometry, veterinary, medical). It combines a cloud phone system (VoIP), two-way texting, scheduling, reviews, forms, and integrated payments. Developers build apps against Weave via a Developer Platform (dp.getweave.com) authorized with OpenID Connect / OAuth 2.0 (api.weaveconnect.com). Y Combinator (W14); HIPAA, ISO 27001, and SOC 2 Type 2 compliant.
