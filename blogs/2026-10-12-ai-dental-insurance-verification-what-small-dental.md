Artificial intelligence is making waves across the healthcare industry. But if your dental practice isn’t sure about leveraging AI in healthcare, you can consider starting with administrative processes, such as insurance verification. Manual dental insurance verification takes a significant amount of time per patient and, unfortunately, just isn’t reliable.
