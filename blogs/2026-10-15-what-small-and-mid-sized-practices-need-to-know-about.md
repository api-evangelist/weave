When insurance claims are denied, your practice wastes time correcting issues and resubmitting. Taking steps to prevent denied claims is important, but knowing where to start can be overwhelming. Often, denied insurance claims arise from errors in the claim details, coverage issues, and failures to follow payer-specific procedures.
