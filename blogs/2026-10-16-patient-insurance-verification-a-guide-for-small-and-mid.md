Your healthcare practice handles a wide range of administrative processes each day to move patients through the care and billing cycle. One important process is patient insurance verification, which involves confirming a patient’s coverage before their appointment. Inaccurate or incomplete patient insurance information is one of the leading causes of claim denials.
